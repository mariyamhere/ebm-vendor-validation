EBM Supplier Governance, Risk and Compliance Policy (MOCK)
Document GRC-PRC-007 · Version 3.2 · Owner: Procurement and Internal Control · Effective 1 July 2026

This is mock content for a demonstration. It is not an EBM policy.

1. Purpose and scope
This policy sets how a new supplier is screened, scored and approved before a purchase order can be raised. It applies to every supplier of ingredients, packaging, logistics and services. Food-contact suppliers carry the strictest checks because a single bad lot reaches the consumer under our brand.

2. Supplier categories
Raw materials: flour, sugar, edible oils and shortening, cocoa, dairy, flavours and any other ingredient. All are food-contact.
Packaging (food contact): laminates, wrappers, trays and liners that touch product.
Packaging (secondary): cartons, shippers, labels and pallets.
Logistics and warehousing: primary and secondary transport, cold chain, third party warehouses.
Services and indirect: maintenance, IT, consultancy, facility services.

3. Knockout rules
A supplier that triggers any knockout is Blocked. A Blocked supplier cannot be approved unless the Head of Procurement records a written exception with the reason.
K1 — No valid NTN, or the supplier is not on the FBR Active Taxpayer List.
K2 — Food-contact supplier (raw materials or food-contact packaging) without a valid food safety certificate: FSSC 22000, ISO 22000, BRCGS or HACCP. An expired certificate counts as no certificate.
K3 — Ingredient supplier without a valid Halal certificate from PHA, PSQCA or a body they recognise. An expired certificate counts as no certificate.
K4 — The supplier, its directors or a company sharing directors appears on the EBM Restricted Parties list in section 6, or on a UN, OFAC or NACTA sanctions list.
K5 — A relationship between the supplier's owners or directors and an EBM employee that the supplier did not disclose on the form.
K6 — Credible evidence of child labour, forced labour or bonded labour.

4. Scoring criteria
Each criterion is scored 0 to 5. The weighted score is the sum of (score ÷ 5 × weight) and runs from 0 to 100.
C1 — Legal and tax compliance (weight 15)
5: NTN, STRN and SECP registration all present and consistent. 3: NTN present, STRN or SECP missing with a reasonable explanation. 1: registrations inconsistent or unverifiable.
C2 — Food safety and quality certification (weight 20)
5: FSSC 22000 or BRCGS, valid for 6+ months. 4: ISO 22000 or HACCP. 3: ISO 9001 only, for non-food-contact suppliers. 0: none for a food-contact supplier.
C3 — Financial stability (weight 15)
5: 10+ years trading and turnover above PKR 250 million. 3: 3 to 10 years or turnover PKR 50 to 250 million. 1: under 3 years and turnover below PKR 50 million, or asks for full advance payment.
C4 — Operational capacity and delivery (weight 15)
5: stated monthly capacity covers EBM's volume at least twice over, with named large clients. 3: capacity covers EBM's volume. 1: capacity unclear or below EBM's volume.
C5 — Ethics, labour and environment (weight 10)
5: SMETA or Sedex audit plus ISO 14001. 3: one of the two, or a credible written policy. 1: nothing on record.
C6 — Reputation and public record (weight 15)
5: clear web presence, positive or neutral coverage, no adverse news. 3: little or no web presence, nothing adverse. 2: no web presence and the supplier gave no website. 0: adverse news about fraud, adulteration, litigation or regulatory action.
C7 — Commercial terms and references (weight 10)
5: 60+ day credit terms and verifiable references. 3: 30 day terms. 1: advance payment only, no references.

5. Tiers and approval authority
Tier A, score 80 and above: approve. Category buyer may approve.
Tier B, score 65 to 79: approve with conditions recorded in the decision note.
Tier C, score 50 to 64: send back for remediation; ask the supplier for the missing items.
Tier D, score below 50: reject.
Blocked, any knockout: reject, or approve only with a Head of Procurement exception.
A supplier expected to exceed PKR 50 million in annual spend also needs Head of Procurement sign-off. Decisions are due within 5 working days of the form being received.
